# Pripev

**A family recipe, in their own voice.**

Pripev turns a loved one's real voice telling a family recipe into a personal
time capsule the family can replay, cook from, gift, print and preserve.

Record someone you love describing a family recipe – now, while the
conversation can still happen – or keep a voice message they have already sent.
Their original, unedited recording becomes the heart of a page assembled from
the family's own photographs, cards, handwriting and kitchen details. The recipe
gives the recording a reason to exist and a reason to be played again.

It is not an AI recipe generator and not a website builder. AI works quietly in
the background to transcribe, organise, cut out artifacts and fit typography.
What the customer buys is the feeling, the object, and the trust.

---

### How we build

- **The original stays original.** The unedited source recording is preserved
  byte for byte. A cleaned playback copy may exist; it never replaces or hides
  the source.
- **The recipe remains as told.** Formatting makes it readable. We do not sell
  correction, and we never invent a measurement that was not spoken.
- **No synthetic loved one.** We do not clone, regenerate, or make new speech in
  anyone's voice.
- **Culture is composed, not themed.** The family's own material leads the
  design. Software does not assign a stereotype from a language or a country.
- **The family can leave.** Originals, transcripts, structured recipes and a
  durable export are always available. Preservation is a tested engineering
  practice, not the word "forever" in marketing copy.
