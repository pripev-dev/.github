# Pripev

**A family recipe, in their own voice.**

</div>

Pripev turns a loved one's real voice telling a family recipe into a personal time
capsule the family can replay, cook from, gift, print and preserve.

Record someone you love describing a family recipe – now, while the conversation can
still happen – or keep a voice message they have already sent. Their original,
unedited recording becomes the heart of a page assembled from the family's own
photographs, cards, handwriting and kitchen details. The recipe gives the recording a
reason to exist, and a reason to be played again.

It is not an AI recipe generator and not a website builder. AI works quietly in the
background to transcribe, organise, cut out artifacts and fit typography. What the
customer buys is the feeling, the object, and the trust.

---

### How we build

- **The original stays original.** The unedited source recording is preserved byte for
  byte. A cleaned playback copy may exist; it never replaces or hides the source.
- **The recipe remains as told.** Formatting makes it readable. We do not sell
  correction, and we never invent a measurement that was not spoken. Where a recording
  is unclear, the page says so and asks the family – it does not quietly fill the gap.
- **No synthetic loved one.** We do not clone, regenerate, or make new speech in
  anyone's voice, and we do not infer age, health or origin from how someone sounds.
- **Culture is composed, not themed.** The family's own material leads the design.
  Software does not assign a stereotype from a language or a country.
- **Private by default.** A capsule is private unless a family deliberately publishes
  it. Sharing a link with relatives is access inside that private state, not
  publication.
- **The family can leave.** Originals, transcripts, structured recipes and a durable
  export are always available. Preservation is a tested engineering practice –
  checksums, independent copies, restore drills – not the word "forever" in marketing
  copy.

---

### What we are building

**A capsule.** One recording, the family's own images, and a page composed from them
that works on a phone in a kitchen, on a desktop, and out of a printer.

**A document that outlives us.** Every capsule is a schema-versioned file plus
content-addressed media, exportable as an archive with its own viewer, so a family can
reopen it without asking our permission or hoping we still exist.

**An editor a person can use.** Move a block, move a cutout, undo. No design tool to
learn, and no template that makes one family's page look like another's.
